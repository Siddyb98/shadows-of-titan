SHADOWS OF TITAN - ARCHIVE UNLOCK NOTES
========================================
Private development reference. This file is not imported by the app and is not in public/.

LOGIN / CALLSIGNS
-----------------
The credential gate accepts any non-empty callsign with at least 3 characters.
Calls are normalized to uppercase and limited to 24 characters.
Reserved authority callsign: AAN-000
Authority callsign grants VOID / C4 clearance and full archive unlocks.
Auth storage: sot-auth-v1
Progression storage: sot-progress-v1-{CALLSIGN}
Default clearance after login: CITIZEN / C0

CLEARANCE TIERS
---------------
C0  CITIZEN  - public archive sections
C1  ECHELON  - personnel and selected records
C2  APEX     - Codex and restricted dossiers
C3  COUNCIL  - Council-only records and high-security personnel
C4  VOID     - highest tier; granted by reserved authority callsign AAN-000

NOTES
-----
- Fragment keys are case-insensitive at entry because FragmentLock uppercases input.
- Failed fragment attempts add an access violation and impose a timed lockout.
- Personnel archive IDs are route IDs; codenames are the visible archive names.
- EKKROS is the only current personnel entry at legacy VOID/C4 clearance.
- The existing app has no server-side authentication or real user accounts.
